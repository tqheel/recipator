Step 1: Choose Your Flavor

Step 2: Ingredients

Always on hand

  • 1-1.5 lb pork tenderloin
  • Neutral oil (vegetable, canola, or olive)
  • Kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder (or 1 fresh clove, minced)
  • Instant-read thermometer

For your selected recipe

  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • Dijon mustard (or yellow mustard)
  • 1 tbsp vinegar (cider, white, or rice)
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary or thyme
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika (smoked or regular)
  • 3 tbsp honey, maple syrup, or fruit jam
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Pinch red pepper flakes (optional)

Step 3: Mix & Apply

Whisk the Marinade

In a bowl or zip-top bag, whisk together 1/4 cup soy sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp Dijon (or yellow) mustard, 1 tbsp vinegar, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1/2 tsp black pepper, and 2 tbsp oil.

Marinate

Add the tenderloin, turn to coat completely, and refrigerate 1-8 hours. Turn once halfway through if using a bowl.

Pat Dry Before Searing

Remove from the marinade and blot the surface lightly so it sears instead of steaming on the griddle.

Make the Paste

Mix 2 tbsp Dijon mustard with 1 tsp dried rosemary or thyme (crushed between your fingers), 1 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, and 1 tbsp oil into a spreadable paste.

Rub It On

Coat the tenderloin evenly on all sides with the paste, pressing gently so it adheres.

Rest Briefly

Let it sit at room temperature 10-15 minutes while the griddle and grill preheat — no long marinating needed.

Combine the Rub

Mix 1 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp brown sugar, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper in a small bowl.

Coat the Tenderloin

Pat the pork dry, drizzle lightly with oil, then press the dry rub evenly over the whole surface.

Rest Before Cooking

Let it sit 15-30 minutes at room temperature (or rub ahead and refrigerate up to a day for deeper flavor).

Whisk the Marinade

Whisk 3 tbsp honey, maple syrup, or melted jam with 2 tbsp Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tbsp vinegar, 1 clove minced garlic (or 1 tsp garlic powder), and a pinch of red pepper flakes if using.

Marinate

Coat the tenderloin in a bag or covered dish and refrigerate 30 minutes to 4 hours.

Pat Dry Before Searing

Blot off excess marinade — this one is sugary and will scorch if left dripping wet on a hot griddle.

⚠️ Sugar Watch: This marinade has enough sugar to scorch at max heat. Sear on medium-high rather than the griddle's highest setting, and keep an eye on color the last 30 seconds per side.

Step 4: Common Method — Griddle Sear + Grill Finish

Same for every flavor above — this is what makes tonight's cook consistent no matter which one you picked.

Preheat Both Surfaces

Preheat the Weber Slate griddle to high (or dedicate one zone high). Preheat the gas grill with one zone left off or on low for an indirect finishing area.

Sear on the Griddle

Place the tenderloin on the hot griddle. Sear 2-3 minutes per side, rolling to hit all sides including the ends, until a deep brown crust forms.

Move to Indirect Heat

Transfer the seared tenderloin to the gas grill's indirect (unlit or low) zone. Close the lid.

Finish to Temperature

Cook until an instant-read thermometer reads 135-140°F in the thickest part. Carryover heat will carry it to a safe 145°F while resting.

Rest and Slice

Tent loosely with foil and rest 5-8 minutes before slicing into medallions.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Tenderloins are lean — pulling them at 135-140°F off the grill (before rest) is the difference between juicy and dry.
  • The griddle's flat, hard contact gives a more even crust than open grates — don't skip the sear step even if you're finishing on the grill anyway.
  • Any of the marinades above can be simmered for a couple of minutes and brushed on again during the grill-finish stage as a glaze — just don't reuse marinade that touched raw pork without boiling it first.
  • If your pack has two tenderloins, run a second flavor from the dropdown for variety from one shopping trip.
