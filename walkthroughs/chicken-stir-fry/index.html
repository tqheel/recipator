CHICKEN STIR-FRY

High-Heat Precision & Balanced Aromatics

← Back to Recipes

Information

Prep Time
20 min
Cook Time
10 min
Difficulty
High
Servings
2-3

I. Preparation & Mise en Place

Rice

Rinse white rice until runoff is clear. Combine with water (1:1.5 ratio). Boil, reduce to low simmer, cover, and cook for 15–18 minutes. Keep covered.

Chicken (Velveting)

Slice chicken into thin strips against the grain. Toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, and 1 tsp cornstarch. Let sit for 15+ minutes.

Vegetables

Slice mushrooms (5mm), cut broccoli into small florets, julienne carrots into matchsticks, and slice bell peppers into strips.

Aromatics & Sauce

Mince 3 cloves garlic and 1 tbsp ginger. Whisk 120ml chicken stock, 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp sugar, and 1 tsp cornstarch.

II. Sequential Cooking Guide

Sauté Mushrooms

Heat 1 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add mushrooms, cook until browned. Remove and set aside.

Sear Chicken

Increase heat to high. Add 1 tbsp oil. Sear chicken for 60s without moving, then toss until opaque. Remove and set aside.

Stir-Fry Hard Vegetables

Add 1 tsp oil if needed. Add broccoli and carrots. Stir-fry for 2 min. Add 2 tbsp water and cover for 60s to steam.

Add Peppers & Aromatics

Add bell peppers, toss for 1 min. Create a well, add garlic and ginger. Sauté for 15s until fragrant.

Integration & Glazing

Return chicken and mushrooms. Give sauce a final stir and pour over. Toss for 45–60s until glossy. Serve immediately over rice.
