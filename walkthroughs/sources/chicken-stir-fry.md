Transitioning to white rice necessitates a shorter cooking duration and offers a more neutral, absorbent starch base for the stir-fry sauce. 

The following guide outlines the end-to-end execution of a chicken stir fry with mushrooms, carrots, broccoli, and bell peppers.

## I. Preparation and Staging (Mise en Place)
Effective wok cooking requires all ingredients to be prepared prior to ignition, as the high-heat phase proceeds rapidly.

1.  **Rice:** Rinse the white rice under cold water until the runoff is clear to remove excess surface starch. Combine with water in a 1:1.5 ratio. Bring to a boil, reduce to a low simmer, cover, and cook for 15–18 minutes. Keep covered until serving.
2.  **Chicken (Velveting):** Slice the chicken into uniform, thin strips against the grain. In a bowl, toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry), and 1 tsp cornstarch. Let sit for at least 15 minutes.
3.  **Vegetables:** * Slice **mushrooms** into 5mm thick pieces.
    * Cut **broccoli** into small, bite-sized florets.
    * Julienne **carrots** into thin matchsticks.
    * Slice **bell peppers** into strips.
4.  **Aromatics & Sauce:** Mince 3 cloves of garlic and 1 tbsp of fresh ginger. In a separate jar, whisk 120ml chicken stock, 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp sugar, and 1 tsp cornstarch.

## II. Sequential Cooking Guide
1.  **Sauté Mushrooms:** Heat 1 tbsp of high-smoke-point oil (e.g., peanut or vegetable) in the wok over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms and cook until browned and moisture has evaporated. Remove and set aside.
2.  **Sear Chicken:** Increase heat to high. Add 1 tbsp of oil. Add chicken in a single layer. Let sear for 60 seconds without moving to develop a crust, then toss until opaque. Remove and set aside with the mushrooms.
3.  **Stir-Fry Hard Vegetables:** If the wok is dry, add 1 tsp of oil. Add broccoli and carrots. Stir-fry for 2 minutes. Add 2 tbsp of water and cover with a lid for 60 seconds to steam-soften the broccoli.
4.  **Add Peppers and Aromatics:** Remove the lid, add bell peppers, and toss for 1 minute. Create a small well in the center; add garlic and ginger. Sauté for 15 seconds until fragrant.
5.  **Integration:** Return the chicken and mushrooms to the wok. 
6.  **Glazing:** Give the sauce mixture a final stir and pour it over the ingredients. Toss continuously for 45–60 seconds. The sauce will transition from opaque to a translucent, glossy glaze that coats the components.
7.  **Service:** Serve immediately over the steamed white rice.

## III. Grocery List

### **Proteins and Grains**
* **Chicken:** 1 lb (approx. 450g) boneless, skinless breast or thighs.
* **Rice:** Long-grain white rice (e.g., Jasmine or Basmati).

### **Produce**
* **Broccoli:** 1 medium head or 1 bag of florets.
* **Carrots:** 2 medium.
* **Mushrooms:** 8 oz (approx. 225g) Cremini or White Button.
* **Bell Peppers:** 1 large (Red or Yellow for color contrast).
* **Aromatics:** 1 bulb of Garlic and 1 piece of fresh Ginger root.

### **Pantry Staples (Verify Stock)**
* **Oils:** High-smoke-point oil (Peanut, Grapeseed, or Vegetable).
* **Liquid Seasoning:** Soy sauce and Oyster sauce.
* **Cooking Wine:** Shaoxing wine or Dry Sherry.
* **Thickener:** Cornstarch.
* **Broth:** Chicken stock or broth.
* **Sugar:** Granulated white sugar or honey.
