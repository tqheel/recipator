🧀 Southern Buttermilk Cheese Biscuits

The ultimate flaky, buttery, and cheesy comfort food

← Back to Walkthroughs

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Total Time: 30 minutes
Yields: 10-12 biscuits

Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-Purpose Flour
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Soda
  • 2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 6 tbsp (3/4 stick) Unsalted Butter, frozen
  • 1 cup Sharp Cheddar Cheese, freshly grated
  • 3/4 to 1 cup Full-Fat Buttermilk, ice cold
  • 2 tbsp Melted Butter (for brushing)

Instructions

  1. Preheat and Prepare
    Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C). For the best crust, place a cast-iron skillet inside as it heats, or grease a baking sheet.
  2. Grate the Frozen Butter
    Place the flour in a large chilled bowl. Use a box grater to shred the frozen butter directly into the flour. Gently toss to coat the butter bits in flour.
  3. Add the Cheese
    Add the freshly grated cheddar cheese to the flour mixture. Quickly toss with your hands until the cheese is evenly distributed and coated in flour.
  4. Form the Dough
    Create a well in the center of the dry ingredients. Pour in 3/4 cup of the ice-cold buttermilk. Using a fork, gently draw the flour from the edges into the center until a shaggy, sticky dough forms. Add more buttermilk if needed.
  5. Laminate for Layers
    Turn the dough onto a floured surface. Pat it into a rectangle. Fold the dough into thirds like a letter. Rotate 90 degrees, pat down, and repeat this process 3-4 times. This creates the signature flaky layers.
  6. Cut the Biscuits
    Pat the dough to a 1-inch thickness. Use a floured 2.5-inch biscuit cutter and press straight down. Do not twist the cutter, as this seals the edges and prevents the rise!
  7. Bake to Golden Perfection
    Place the biscuits in the skillet or on the baking sheet so they are touching. This helps them rise taller. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the tops are golden brown.
  8. The Finishing Touch
    Immediately upon removing from the oven, brush the tops of the hot biscuits with melted butter. Serve warm!

💡 Pro Tips for Perfect Biscuits

  • Keep Everything Cold: Warm fats lead to flat biscuits. If your kitchen is hot, chill your bowl and flour before starting.
  • Freshly Grated Cheese: Avoid pre-shredded cheese; it's coated in potato starch which prevents it from melting perfectly into the dough.
  • Don't Overwork: Handle the dough as little as possible. The heat from your hands can melt the butter.
  • The Shoulder-to-Shoulder Rule: Biscuits rise better when they have "neighbors" to lean on. Make sure they touch in the pan!
